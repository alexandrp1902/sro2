## splash-login

> Use case: stylized-concept. Single production space RPG title-screen illustration, 1920x1080 landscape 16:9. Detailed semi-realistic game painting matching worn blue-gray industrial spaceship sprites: gunmetal hulls, scratched pale steel panels, dark navy space, restrained cyan practical lights and amber warning details, clear readable midtones, no neon overload. Cinematic wide shot. The CENTER of the frame is deliberately the calmest and darkest part — open space, distant dust and faint glow only, no ships, no explosions and no bright light there — because an interface panel is placed over it; all loud action, light and detail live in the left, right and upper thirds, and the lower third stays dark. The frame is also center-cropped to a tall 9:16 phone screen, so the central 35% of the width must still read as a finished picture, never as empty flat black. No text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, frame, border, UI, HUD, cursor or signature. Epic space battle in deep space above a planet, seen from a cinematic three-quarter angle, not top-down. LEFT THIRD: a wedge-hulled ranger patrol corvette and two heavier ranger cruisers in navy-and-white livery with light-blue chevrons sweep inward, firing thin white-cyan railgun streaks and launching chunky dark torpedoes with bright orange exhaust trails. RIGHT THIRD: ragged pirate raiders welded from mismatched scavenged plates, rust-red and soot-black armour with scorched patches, return fire with amber tracers; one pirate hull is breaking apart in an orange-white explosion with a dark smoke ring and slowly tumbling debris, another trails burning venting gas. UPPER AREA: a small cold white star with a tight corona rim-lighting every hull from the upper left, faint navy and violet nebula dust. LOWER THIRD: the curved limb of a blue-green world with thin cloud bands and a thin atmospheric glow along the horizon, dark and low in contrast. MIDDLE OF THE FRAME: quiet dark space between the two formations — only the planet limb, sparse cold stars and faint dust, no ships and no flashes there. Scale reads large: hulls are small and sharp at distance, and the battle fills the frame.

## splash-cockpit

Отступление от STYLE-SPLASH: вместо «game painting» просили фотореализм, кабина занимает нижнюю треть и края, центр — открытый космос под панель входа.

> Use case: photorealistic-concept. Single production space RPG title-screen illustration, 1920x1080 landscape 16:9. Photorealistic, cinematic, physically plausible rendering, like a still from a high-budget science-fiction film, matching a worn blue-gray industrial spaceship aesthetic: gunmetal structure, scratched pale steel panels, dark navy space, restrained cyan practical lights and amber warning details, clear readable midtones, no neon overload. FIRST-PERSON VIEW FROM THE PILOT SEAT INSIDE A SPACESHIP COCKPIT looking out through a huge wide panoramic windshield into deep space. FOREGROUND, lower third and both side edges: the worn cockpit interior — dark scratched steel window frame struts, the top of an instrument console with dimly glowing cyan and amber gauges, small blinking indicator lamps, a scuffed control yoke, soft reflections of the console lights on the glass, faint dust and scratches on the windshield, everything dark and low in contrast. LEFT THIRD outside the glass: a large gas giant with detailed cream, ochre and pale-blue cloud bands, a thin bright atmospheric rim lit by the star, one small moon in front of it. RIGHT THIRD outside the glass: a distant worn blue-gray industrial space station or heavy cargo ship, small and sharp, catching the same light, and a dense drifting field of sunlit dust and small tumbling rocks. UPPER AREA: a faint cold navy-and-violet nebula and thousands of sharp pinpoint stars, a small cold white star far to the upper left rim-lighting the planet and the station, no lens flare over the center. THE CENTER OF THE FRAME is deliberately the calmest and darkest part: open deep space with only sparse cold stars, faint nebula dust and gentle gradient, no planet, no ships, no bright light and no console there, because an interface panel is placed over it. The frame is also center-cropped to a tall 9:16 phone screen, so the central 35% of the width must still read as a finished picture with subtle depth and detail, never as empty flat black. No people, no hands, no text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, frame, border, UI, HUD, cursor or signature.
