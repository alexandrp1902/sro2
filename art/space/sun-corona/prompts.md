# Generation prompts

Пачка I из `art/next-art-requests.md`: кольца короны звезды. Четыре слоя, которые код кладёт поверх спрайта звезды и анимирует масштабом и прозрачностью. Центр каждого кольца обязан быть пустым. PNG сгенерированы в партии 2026-09-22 (завершена 2026-09-23); замечания — в общем отчёте.

## suns-corona-inner.png

Use case: stylized-concept. ONE isolated stellar corona RING sprite for a top-down 2D space RPG, 1024x1024 canvas with genuinely TRANSPARENT alpha background, not a checkerboard drawing. This is an ANNULUS, a ring of glowing plasma: the middle of the image is COMPLETELY EMPTY and fully transparent, there is no star, no filled disc, no core and no glow in the centre. The ring is exactly centered on the canvas and perfectly concentric, so the engine can scale and rotate it around the star sprite. Near-white and only faintly tinted, so the engine can tint it per star colour. Even brightness all the way around the ring, no single bright spot, no lens flare, no cross-shaped or star-shaped rays, no starfield, no planet, no text, letters, numbers, logo, frame or watermark. Alpha falls to exactly zero before the canvas border on the outside and to exactly zero toward the centre on the inside, with soft gradients and no hard cut or outline anywhere. Tight chromosphere ring hugging the star: a narrow bright annulus, inner edge crisp, outer edge soft; the transparent hole is about 72% of the canvas width and the luminous band only about 7% of the width thick, with fine short plasma fibrils standing radially in it.
