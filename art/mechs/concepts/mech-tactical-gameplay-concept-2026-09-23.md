Это визуальный концепт будущего экрана, не скриншот работающего режима. Числа иллюстративные; нарисованная дистанция и стрелки направления не полностью согласованы с данными карточки. Карта и спрайты имеют небольшой наклон камеры. При реализации нужно согласовать геометрию клеток, направления и расчёт прогноза с сервером.

## Фактический промпт

Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE polished high-fidelity raster concept of a playable desktop browser game screen for SRO, a turn-based PvE mech tactics mode. This is the actual player-facing game viewport, not a poster, not a monitor photograph, not a design presentation or collage. Landscape 16:9, target 1920x1080 or higher equivalent. All UI labels in clean correctly spelled Russian. Compose as an attractive coherent finished tactical game screenshot that a developer could implement.

GAME MECHANICS TO COMMUNICATE: alternating side activations; player chooses any unactivated mech, not a fixed initiative queue. One movement followed by one weapon attack, no action point pool. Four allied mechs versus four enemies on a square 16x16 battlefield. Mechs have individually damageable BODY, CHASSIS, LEFT ARM, RIGHT ARM. Each arm is a weapon or physical shield. Destroyed arm disables that weapon. Direction/facing matters, shield only protects appropriate direction. Called shot at a chosen part has reduced hit probability. Skills are passive automatic procs, NO ability hotbar, no mana, no AP counters. This moment is a stationary allied autocannon mech previewing a called shot at enemy right arm, not firing yet.

ART / CAMERA: strict near-vertical 90-degree overhead orthographic tactical view, square grid horizontal and vertical on screen, NOT isometric diamond grid. Compact readable dorsal-view bipedal combat mech sprites with shoulder armor, roof sensors, visible small feet and integrated arm weapons. Four allied worn pale steel and blue-gray mechs with cyan/blue identification rings, four hostile dark charcoal and rust-red mechs with restrained red rings. Each mech occupies one cell, distinct silhouettes: light scout, medium cannon+shield selected, heavy, sniper. All mechs clearly grounded and correctly sized in scene. NO giant frontal robot pasted over the map. The battlefield is a weathered desert industrial outpost, warm grey sand, steel floor sections, rusted pipes, container stacks, low concrete barriers, roofs of small industrial buildings, debris, cool shadows, fine dust. Controlled cinematic upper-left light but clear tactical readability. Environmental detail rich, not noisy. No horizon, no sky.
